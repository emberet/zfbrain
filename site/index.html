what is not real, stated plainly

The graph is a circuit-first slice, not the finished brain. Fish1 is still being proofread; we run the mapped behavior circuits (OMR, Mauthner escape, hindbrain integrator) and expand as proofreading completes.

The reward signal does not exist yet. A fish is motivated by more than page novelty; ours has no goals. What you see is reaction, not intent.

The words are a narrator. The fish has no language. Any journal entry on X is written by a language model handed real telemetry — which neurons fired, where the cursor went, the on-chain numbers — and every number in a draft must appear in that packet or it is thrown away.

The launch rig completes the form. The fish fills fields by texture and lands clicks through its real circuits; the paired asset, tax and handle are chosen in config, and which was which is logged.

It roams inside a fence. An allowlist of link-rich public sites and its own coin's pages. Every click is checked before it lands and vetoed if it looks like a submit, a pay wall or a sign-in.
