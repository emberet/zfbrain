> what it is

A real nervous system, not a metaphor

Every connection here was traced from electron-microscope volumes of an actual five-day-old zebrafish. Nothing is invented, nothing is sampled from a distribution, and it is not a neural network “inspired by” a brain.

The Fish1 larval connectome comes from the Lichtman and Engert labs with Google, released CC-BY and still being proofread. Day one runs the circuits the release already mapped — OMR, Mauthner escape, hindbrain integrator — and widens as proofreading completes. Sign comes from each cell's neurotransmitter: vglut2a excites, gad1b inhibits. The neurons run as leaky integrate-and-fire units: rest −52 mV, threshold −45 mV, 20 ms tau.

> how it reaches the world

It sees with a fish's eye and swims with a fish's tail

A screenshot of the live page is sampled into a luminance and optic-flow retina and injected into the retinal ganglion cells. Every neuron in the running graph integrates. The cursor comes back out of the descending groups a larva actually swims with.
